## What these cards establish

The directory includes the [BLS CPI and employment releases](../docs/news_research/macro_tech/candidates.en.md), FOMC decisions, BIS/DOE policy documents, official geopolitical statements, Federal Reserve financial-stability material, and CoreWeave/NVIDIA filings or issuer releases. The [source register](../docs/news_research/macro_tech/SOURCE_REGISTER.md) gives verified primary links and precise limitations. For example, the [BLS October CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_11102026.htm) reports 0.4% seasonally adjusted monthly CPI-U growth and 0.3% for core CPI, while the [BLS employment release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062026.htm) reports 165,000 added nonfarm jobs and 4.1% unemployment. These are published values in the cited archived releases, not surprises against a verified prior consensus. The [Federal Reserve stress-test release](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260624a.htm) reports hypothetical scenario results, not realized bank losses. The [CoreWeave Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000357/crwv-20260807.htm) gives a Term SOFR plus 5.50-percentage-point margin for specified loans, not a 5.50% all-in borrowing rate.

Selection deliberately used source and event criteria, not subsequent stock moves. Even so, a hand-selected set of 12 retrospective cards is not a continuous or representative event panel. Printed BLS embargo and Fed release times are stored separately from independently observed first-public instants, which remain unknown for all cards. There is no dated pre-release consensus for the current cards, so `actual − consensus` is not computed. Source statements, issuer intent, institutional assessments and analyst mechanisms remain distinct. A ticker, when present, is a candidate identifier rather than a checked historical security link.
