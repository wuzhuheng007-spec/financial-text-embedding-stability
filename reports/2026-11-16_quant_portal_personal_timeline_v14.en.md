## What works

Click **查看曲线** on one of the six existing quote cards (TQQQ, QQQ, SOXX, SPY, NVDA or MU). Choose premarket, regular, after hours, overnight or all sessions, then a 30-minute, two-hour, 24-hour or at-most-seven-day cache view. Zoom and **回到最新** preserve a paused reading position. A selected quote point shows its saved price, session, supplier source time, local receipt time and channel in the owner-only page. The SVG is local and uses no third-party chart service. It plots saved supplier **quotes**, not confirmed trades, a complete tick feed or one-minute bars. Its browser requests read the local cache every five seconds and add no moomoo subscription or Jin10 request.

Source-time-unknown snapshots remain in the quote cards but cannot enter the curve. Same-session, same-source-time revisions choose the latest received version; older late packets cannot overwrite newer plotted times. The curve breaks at session changes, recorded connection interruptions and observation gaps over 180 seconds. It never fills missing prices. The response caps displayed points at 500 and labels sampling and scan limits. The underlying quote history is not deleted for this view.

The timeline reuses the existing phrase classifier. It lists directly named company/ETF items and shared macro-topic items with a visible reason, and places a marker only when a supplier publication time is parseable with a timezone. It separately displays the first local receipt. Missing-time news stays in the list. Saved corrections and suspected related reports remain expandable, without treating reprints as independent confirmation. The labels say **Jin10 report, unverified** and describe temporal proximity without claiming causation. Jin10's [official interface guide](https://mcp.jin10.com/app/doc.html) documents flash items and their `time`/`url` fields; the actual saved response shape, not the guide's demo values, supplied this page.

## Actual evidence and limits

The [frozen private-cache audit](../results/quant_portal_personal_timeline_v14_20261116/real_cache_audit_redacted.json) used a private SQLite snapshot (SHA-256 and code/config hashes in the receipt) at **04:01:22 UTC / 12:01:22 Asia/Shanghai**. It counted 4,204 saved session-quote rows, 1,220 retained raw responses and 196 Jin10 items. All six symbols had four named session fields, but the premarket, regular and after-hours records each represented only one distinct older source timestamp per symbol. Overnight source-time coverage on the saved sample ran from about 03:08 to 04:01 UTC. For TQQQ it was 03:08:04–04:01:13 UTC; for NVDA 03:08:24–04:01:20 UTC. Other sessions were not observed continuously in this run. NVDA had 2,215 unique valid points in the seven-day view, of which 498 original points were displayed after sampling. The display is therefore explicitly a sampled cache view.

The same audit matched 18 sampled plotted points to saved symbol/session/source/receipt/price rows and checked 18 displayed news publication/first-receipt pairs against saved versions. This is a targeted source readback, not proof that the supplier reported every market event. The earlier private backup had 2,624 quote rows, 113 raw responses and 173 news items. The prior v1.3 retention had already removed originals for some old quote rows: 2,890 rows in the frozen v1.4 audit lacked a retained original response. The revised policy retains originals supporting recent 48-hour curve rows, currently visible session cells and 100 further recent responses; it cannot recreate old originals. All 1,185 rows received since the actual 03:48 UTC worker upgrade had retained raw responses in the audit. Quote-history pruning remains off and future disk growth needs monitoring.

The saved [SPY reconciliation](../results/quant_portal_personal_timeline_v14_20261116/spy_window_reconciliation_redacted.json) resolves the old 11-versus-10 count. **Eleven** overnight source-time advances belong to the 03:08:53–03:13:53 UTC development window (61 cache reads); **ten** belong to the separate 03:17:00–03:22:01 UTC frozen-code window (60 reads). These are distinct five-minute observations, not competing scores. Both private receipt SHA-256 values are in the reconciliation.

In [three local cache samples](../results/quant_portal_personal_timeline_v14_20261116/local_two_cycle_observation_redacted.json) five seconds apart, NVDA's latest source time advanced at both intervals, while TQQQ's did not advance in that ten-second span. This does not establish a supplier fault or zero delay. The [browser acceptance](../results/quant_portal_personal_timeline_v14_20261116/browser_visual_redacted.json) used the existing signed-in owner Edge page: the desktop curve, news markers, session switch, empty two-hour premarket state, zoom, latest button and news click were observed. The first 390px layout crowded controls; after a CSS correction, the controls stacked and only the chart scrolled horizontally inside its card. No private screenshot was published. The visible page still shows the preserved historical fact cards, subscription controls and Jin10 feed. The [runtime receipt](../results/quant_portal_personal_timeline_v14_20261116/runtime_redacted.json) records one local account, six watchlist items, one settings row and 14 historical fact cards after restart, with both portal and feed running.
