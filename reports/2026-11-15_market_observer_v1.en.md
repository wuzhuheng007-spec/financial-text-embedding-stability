## Research aim and result boundary

The observer is intended to preserve source reports, read-only market observations, decisions made before an evaluation window, and later outcomes so that extraction and market judgments can be reviewed separately. This stage delivered a local engineering prototype and a bounded authenticated moomoo query. **Continuous quote push, real pre-window predictions, mature outcomes, model learning and historical financial research acceptance were not established.** It placed no trades and read no positions, balances or orders.

## What the bounded provider probe shows

The fresh CLI session made **one** security-information call for the fixed twelve-symbol pool and **one** quote call for verified `US.NVDA`; the business responses reported success and 12 and 1 rows respectively. The quote's own data time is `2026-11-13T21:00:00Z`. The probe took place on Sunday in Asia/Shanghai, so this saved Friday quote is not evidence of a current stream. The row did not expose a separate delay flag, leaving account delay status unresolved. The wrapper logged the overall CLI session interval; per-tool wall-clock times were not mechanically captured and are recorded as unavailable. Raw `initialize` and `tools/list` RPC receipts were also unavailable; the 104 bindings are an exposed fresh-CLI registry observation, not a raw negotiated-list archive. [Redacted connection receipt](../results/market_observer_v1_20261115/moomoo_connection_receipt.json).

Official [MCP quick start](https://open.moomoo.com/mcp-docs/quick-start) and [authentication](https://open.moomoo.com/mcp-docs/authentication) document the endpoint and scopes. The separately documented [WebSocket subscription](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/push/subscribe) requires its own authenticated, entitled and quota-bounded path. The MCP read success does not prove that path is usable. [Account market permissions](https://open.moomoo.com/api/overview/api-authority) and [subscription quota documentation](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/quotas/subscription) are recorded as source requirements, not account grants. Jin10 polling and retention rights remain unconfirmed, so this version only replays already saved private data.

## Local operation and next gate

From the repository root, run `python -m scripts.market_observer_v1.cli start --config configs/market_observer_v1.example.json`; use the same prefix with `status` or `stop`. The example configuration intentionally starts in `NO_FEED` mode. It yields a truthful service and saved-data page, **not live collection**. The acceptance process was stopped; the [service receipt](../results/market_observer_v1_20261115/service_lifecycle_redacted.json) records its final state. The page shows absent quotes as missing rather than zero and distinguishes source time from local receipt; private SQLite also retains persistence time. [Public blank page](../results/market_observer_v1_20261115/dashboard_public_template.html) contains no licensed provider text.

Next, establish one authorized standalone read-only push method and its account-specific quote entitlement, quota, retention and symbol subscription rules. Freeze the actual trading calendar and price/adjustment basis, then run a bounded open-market connection acceptance. Confirm Jin10 continuous-call conditions before enabling incremental news polling. Only future events captured before their decision window can yield real observer predictions; later matured bars may then support descriptive evaluation. The six reused shortfacts tasks and this saved-data replay cannot demonstrate general extraction quality, an alpha effect or profitability. Formal M5 admission, returns labels and backtesting remain `NOT_RUN`.
