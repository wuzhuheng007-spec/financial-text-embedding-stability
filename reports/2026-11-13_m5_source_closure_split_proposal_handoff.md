## Proposed price rule and source boundary

The public [CRSP calculation guide](https://indexes.morningstar.com/api/docs/6a454f3183e511651648cf28) supports adjusted price as `P/C`, ex-date adjustment, and the simple-split factor `q=1+f`; its two-for-one example uses `f=1`, `q=2`. The [proposal](../docs/M5_SIMPLE_SPLIT_PRICE_PROPOSAL_20261113.md) derives `A_B(t)=P(t)/prod(q_e: t<e<=B)` for `t<=B`, with the inverse unit conversion for later `t`. Its rejection policy covers unconfirmed/complex or same-day multiple actions, invalid factors, unknown gaps, missing fixed-session traded closes, and unresolved duplicate keys. No actual return or mask was calculated. A `DlyFacPrc`, `DlyRetx`, `DisFacPr`, or cumulative factor is not treated as a split-only shortcut. Ordinary-cash classification also needs saved-release support before its proposed price-only handling.

For the conditional complete-window direction, a missing required close or unresolved exit affecting a required price observation remains unscored with its reason, while original company/event denominators and attrition remain visible. No successor, quote, next-available price, zero, or terminal-value substitution is made. A known-terminal formula is needed if a different terminal-substitution estimand is proposed, and filtered exit coverage remains unresolved even without such a formula. This clarification is a proposal, not a changed financial result or accepted full M5 rule.
