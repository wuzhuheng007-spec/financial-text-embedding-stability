## A1: short-tail weight mechanism

The prior M6 run retained document vectors and chunk maps, but not each chunk vector. This M7 run therefore recomputed chunk vectors from the exact fixed model and five saved Item 1 texts. It first reproduced all four old document-vector and cosine arms, then used the same chunk vectors for the new interventions. Across both chunk sizes all non-tail chunks were full; therefore weights `[1, ..., tail_length / body_length]` are exactly proportional to `[body_length, ..., tail_length]`. Tail-only adjustment and body-token weighting are **one algebraically equivalent arm**, not two independent findings.

| Body tokens/chunk | Method versus equal pooling | Upper-triangle Spearman (10 dependent pairs) | Max absolute off-diagonal cosine change | Companies with changed Top-2 membership |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 128 | Body-token weighted / tail-only ratio | 0.987879 | 0.004487 | None |
| 128 | Drop final chunk, diagnostic only | 0.951515 | 0.016288 | None |
| 192 | Body-token weighted / tail-only ratio | 0.987879 | 0.007236 | MSFT |
| 192 | Drop final chunk, diagnostic only | 0.987879 | 0.008552 | MSFT |

At 192, MSFT has 8,489 body tokens, 45 chunks and a 41-token tail. The final chunk has normalized weight `1/45 = 0.0222222` under equal pooling and `41/8489 = 0.00482978` under body-token weighting. MSFT's equal-pooling Top-2 is GOOGL/META with rank-2-versus-rank-3 cosine gap `0.00119002`; under body-token weighting it is GOOGL/AMZN with gap `0.00360910`; under final-chunk deletion it is GOOGL/AMZN with gap `0.00343063`. The other four companies retain their Top-2 membership in these 192-token arms. This identifies an observed local rank sensitivity to tail treatment; deleting the tail removes source text and is not an improvement proposal. Every deleted span has token/character offsets and a SHA-256 matched to the saved Item 1 text in `tail_v2/tail_weight_diagnostics.json`. The raw source and chunk-map trace should be consulted before any semantic interpretation.

The same-team checker rehashed 25 recorded A1 outputs and recomputed chunk-to-document aggregations, cosine matrices, ranks/margins and removed-span hashes from the saved chunk vectors/maps; it reported 241/241 arithmetic/hash checks passing. ## A2: BM25 length correction before and after cosine normalization

The M6 lexical fit uses exactly these five Item 1 documents, `[a-z0-9]+` lowercase tokens, vocabulary size 3,023, average length 4,469.4 lexical tokens, `k1=1.2` and `b={0,0.75,1}`. IDF is `log(1+(N-df+0.5)/(df+0.5))`, and each document-term weight is `IDF × f(k1+1)/(f+k1(1-b+bL/avgL))`. The directed BM25 query-to-document score sums weights over **unique** query terms; it is not a symmetric similarity or covariance matrix. The separate BM25 term-weight cosine L2 normalizes each document term vector. Equal peer scores are ordered by ticker; Spearman uses exact-value average ranks and retains null if undefined.

| b versus b=0 | Raw term-vector change | Term-weight cosine, max off-diagonal change | Cosine upper Spearman | Directed score, max off-diagonal change | Ranking observations |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.75 | Pre-L2 norms and term weights changed; see 15-row CSV | 0.001447 | 1.000000 | 43.0917 | Cosine peer order unchanged; directed full peer order changed for AMZN, GOOGL, MSFT |
| 1.00 | Pre-L2 norms and term weights changed; see 15-row CSV | 0.001899 | 1.000000 | 54.7297 | Cosine peer order unchanged; directed full peer order changed for AMZN, GOOGL, MSFT |

For each company and b, the diagnostic fits a single positive document scale `α=(w₀·w_b)/(w₀·w₀)` on the identical nonzero term support, then reports `||w_b−αw₀||₂/||w_b||₂`. At b=1 the real-document residuals range from `0.00907` (GOOGL) to `0.09518` (AMZN); the weights are therefore not exactly a document-wide scalar. Their normalized directions change slightly (direction cosines 0.99546–0.99996). L2 normalization suppresses a common positive scale, but cannot in general erase frequency-dependent direction changes. Positive software controls confirm exact L2 invariance under positive scalar multiplication, exact document-scalar behavior when all within-document frequencies are one, and a mixed-frequency counterexample. The controls use synthetic tokens and appear in `bm25/synthetic_software_controls.json`, not in the real-company table. Unchanged cosine ranks in five documents do **not** imply that BM25 length correction is ineffective. This run does not tune or nominate a best b.

The same-team checker verified the eight recorded A2 output hashes and separately recalculated weights, directions, directed scores, saved matrices, scale residuals and plot parsing. Numerical agreement with M6 saved matrices was within floating-point precision. It did not create independent relevance labels or an external audit.
