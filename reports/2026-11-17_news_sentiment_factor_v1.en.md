# News sentiment factor v1: historical coverage and gated input panel

Run date: 17 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai. The frozen development window is 1 April 2025–31 July 2026. News is the proposed factor; prices and volume are controls. **No A/B/C/D estimator was fitted and no Pearson IC, Rank IC, backtest, or return claim was computed.** See the [plan–actual ledger](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/plan_execution.csv), [protocol configuration](../configs/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117.json), and [redacted coverage summary](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/coverage_summary_redacted.json).

The saved, hash-checked moomoo US trading calendar has 335 dates. Six saved unadjusted daily series cover NVDA, MU, TQQQ, QQQ, SOXX, and SPY on those dates; this is price-file coverage, not proof of correct corporate-action treatment or contemporaneous bar availability. The saved official source register has 60 documents within the window, grouped into 20 NVDA, 19 MU, and 11 macro event groups. These are stored-document counts, not a complete historical news archive. The [monthly matrix](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/coverage_matrix.csv) contains 96 symbol-month rows. Earlier corporate-action checking covered only TQQQ, NVDA, and MU within its stated scope.

The private 670-row NVDA/MU company-day panel retains source hashes, an unadjusted same-session close/open ratio, volume, and a **partial saved-issuer-document count** under a conservative 20-trading-day date proxy. It keeps full-archive news count, tone, uncertainty, and novelty missing, and does not calculate labels. A zero in the partial issuer subset does not mean there was no news. The proxy admits an official announcement only after one complete subsequent US regular trading session; it does not prove historical first availability. The strict historical-availability branch remains separate. [Panel manifest](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/panel_manifest_redacted.json), [time gate](../scripts/news_sentiment_factor_v1/protocol.py).

The personal Jin10 cache was received in November 2026 and is not a historical archive; its snapshot contained zero news with publication dates in the study window. A bounded Jin10 `search_news` probe returned zero for NVDA, zero for Micron, and ten on the first page for 美光, seven of which fell within the requested window; `has_more=true`. Search hits cannot establish archive completeness, and no article body was saved from the probes. [Redacted source probe](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/source_probe_redacted.json). Prior April–May validation was reused repeatedly and June–July test labels were previously viewed, so neither can be represented as a new independent test; see the [saved exposure record](../results/market_observer_historical_20261115/test_contamination_redacted.json).

The frozen protocol uses NVDA/MU ordinary shares, ETFs only as separate market context, a 17:00 New York retrospective date-proxy cutoff, the next regular-session open as proposed execution reference, a five-trading-day primary horizon, and one/20-day auxiliaries. Any label crossing a split boundary must be excluded. Point-in-time price availability and action-adjusted outcome prices remain unverified, so even the price-only comparison was not fitted in this factor run. A/B/C/D and cross-sectional IC remain gated by qualified news coverage, event deduplication, source versions, and enough valid stocks per day.

Two English-language candidates were source-checked but not run: the [Notre Dame Loughran–McDonald dictionary](https://sraf.nd.edu/loughranmcdonald-master-dictionary/) lists a March 2026 update and free academic research use with separate commercial licensing; the [ProsusAI FinBERT model card](https://huggingface.co/ProsusAI/finbert) describes English financial sentiment and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 weights. The recorded FinBERT repository revision is `0bc2ac10dda6929bcb1f77ccc43c7ec5c424f031`, with a repository update after the study window; its historical availability is not established. Neither model nor lexicon was downloaded or scored here, and Chinese news was not passed to an English classifier. [Source receipt](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v1_20261117/model_sources_redacted.json).

An initial panel draft accidentally placed a whole-month official-event count on each day, which could reveal later events within that month. That field was removed and the panel rebuilt before use. **Whether news content adds information beyond price/volume plus event count remains unknown.** The next dependency is an authorized, versioned historical news archive with audited release/availability times and broader cross-sectional coverage, followed by separate price-timing and corporate-action checks. The current page-commentary model is not a prerequisite for that data work.
