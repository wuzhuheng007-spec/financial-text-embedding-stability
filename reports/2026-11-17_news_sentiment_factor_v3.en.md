## Timing, provenance and limits

The complete SEC submission header acceptance time matched the submissions API timestamp. It is an `SEC_ACCEPTANCE_ANCHOR`, not proof of first website visibility or actual investor receipt. The date proxy waits for one complete regular session after the acceptance date, then enters at the next actual trading-session open. One- and five-session exits count half days. Three of the original eight events had a different five-session exit date from v2 because the three half sessions are counted; old outputs are untouched. Unadjusted opens are used, and known split/dividend dates inside a return window exclude that label. An unknown action response would not be treated as no action. See the [official moomoo history K-line documentation](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/basic-data/history-kline) and [SEC EDGAR access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data).

These are **official issuer-disclosure language** factors. Keyword section assignment can still be wrong; not every scored chunk has semantic review. The normalized source-span audit found 1,728/1,728 exact matches, but source-heading and limited pre-outcome spot checks do not establish full blind review. The comprehensive locator check ran after alignment and is identified as such. Old v2 exhibit byte hashes differ from the newly extracted complete-submission exhibit bytes, so a higher chunk count is not a directly comparable completeness rate. LM dictionary and pinned FinBERT historical point-in-time availability are not established for each 2025 signal date; v2's [version and license register](../results/news_sentiment_factor_v2_20261117/literature_register.json) applies. The FinBERT model card identifies noncommercial licensing for the local research use.

The watchlist is purpose-selected, same-day and same-issuer events are dependent, and 24 fits are not 24 independent experiments. The old eight-event leave-one-out diagnostic changed Pearson by as much as about `1.037` in absolute value, illustrating sensitivity without motivating any deletion. With no complete English media archive, untouched test, strict point-in-time access proof, or prospective label pairing, **a stable incremental text effect over price and count is not established**. Financial input v2.5 remains `REJECTED_CANDIDATE`; no Qwen call, fine-tuning, trading, or return strategy was launched.
