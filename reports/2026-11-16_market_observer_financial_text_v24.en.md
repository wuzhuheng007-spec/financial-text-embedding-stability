# Financial table semantics v2.4: four-table source regression

**Executed 16 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai (UTC+08:00).** This round repaired the binding of true table headers, periods, units and accounting basis. It reused four saved training-period NVIDIA/Micron SEC earnings exhibits. There was no new corpus download, April–July 2026 body or return-label read, model run, or personal-app change. The team had prior validation exposure; these are known development examples, not an independent test.

## Result

All **six known NVIDIA witnesses** now have complete source-bound relationships in the one frozen v2.4 regression, versus **zero of the same six** in the v2.3 source review. The earlier 12 false numeric-row column-header links became **zero** in this four-table check. Wrong period links, wrong unit scopes, false complete declarations, missed numeric cells and newly unverified explicit links were also **zero**. This is a matched small-regression comparison, not a comparison of the old 71-candidate literal-fragment metric with a new structural metric.
