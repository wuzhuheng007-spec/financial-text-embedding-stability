BM25 retains the project's explicit ASCII-alphanumeric lowercase tokenizer, no stopword removal or stemming, unique query terms, positive `log1p((N-df+0.5)/(df+0.5))` IDF, `k1=1.2`, and `b=0.75`. Collection statistics were fitted on **all 166 validated nodes before query filters** (mean lexical length about 110.64 tokens; 1,874 terms). BM25 scores are directional query-to-node relevance. Dense scores are chunk cosine. Hybrid scores are weighted reciprocal-rank fusion with `rrf_k=60` and equal branch weights. Query rewriting and automatic generation were disabled. Company, accession, section, and version constraints were tested across branches; an impossible ticker yielded `NO_MATCH` and zero nodes. These scores are not comparable numerical scales and are not financial returns or covariance.
