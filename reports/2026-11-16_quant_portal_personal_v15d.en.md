## Aim, actual work and limited finding

In v1.5b, eight of the 20 messages passed mechanical checks, but [same-team source review](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15_20261116/semantic_review_redacted.json) rejected all eight. This retry replaced model-written source quotations with evidence span IDs, resolved by the program against the exact saved source version. Intermediate v1.5c saved results for only eight items and 14 recorded completed generations. Its four mechanical completions were [all rejected](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15c_20261116/semantic_review_redacted.json), chiefly because English names drifted, so the run stopped. The earlier redacted receipt undercounted one rejection reason; a [versioned reconciliation](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15c_20261116/semantic_review_reconciliation_redacted.json) recomputed the frequencies from the frozen private review without changing the old receipt. An item-nine request was prepared without a saved response and is not counted as a completed call. The [negative run receipt](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15c_20261116/run_redacted.json) remains unchanged.

Version v1.5d masks an explicitly formatted Chinese speaker name with an opaque placeholder. The model must keep that placeholder in both languages; only the display view deterministically restores spelling from the same source. Raw response, display adaptation and same-team review are separate. The rule covers one explicit speaker pattern, not every entity. [Configuration](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/run_config.json) and [source/code hash manifest](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/input_manifest_redacted.json) were frozen before generation. All 20 full prompts passed the 2,048-token preflight, with a maximum of 922 tokens, according to the [receipt](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/token_preflight_redacted.json).

The [actual v1.5d run](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/run_redacted.json) attempted **20/20 items**, saved **30 real generations**, mechanically completed **11**, and failed **nine**. Same-team item-by-item source review [accepted two and rejected nine](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/semantic_review_redacted.json) of the mechanical completions. Rejections include residual Chinese in English, mistranslated entities, unsupported policy inference, weak relevance and overgeneralization. Acceptance means only that wording is compatible with limited evidence in a supplier report; the underlying Jin10 claims were **not independently fact-checked**. All versions reuse the same development messages, while the evidence contract, name handling and prompt changed together. The shift from zero to two accepted cards cannot be assigned to one change or generalized.

The two accepted display cards were imported into a **separate private SQLite review cache**. They appear only for the signed-in owner when the news version and content hash still match. Other items retain their actual pending, failed or insufficient-evidence status. The continuously running analysis worker still uses the earlier v1.5b contract; v1.5d is a frozen development batch and is not automatically applied to future news. The page preserves the original report and its supplier attribution. At the moment of the [read-only runtime receipt](../results/quant_portal_personal_v15d_20261116/runtime_redacted.json), the local portal returned HTTP 200, six cached quote symbols, ten Top 10 positions and two approved visible cards; moomoo and Jin10 heartbeat states were also recorded. This is a point-in-time local readback, not a visual or long-run acceptance.

The user observed that Jin10 flashes are usually Chinese and moomoo may have English news. A prior [bounded source probe](../results/market_observer_historical_20261115/news_official_redacted.json) did return one moomoo news-search page, but that interface is **not connected to the personal news cache**, and this run did not verify its English share or continued permission. A later bounded access check can preserve source language, original publisher and reprint relationships. A Chinese and an English rendition of the same report must not automatically count as independent corroboration. Current English analyses are marked machine-generated; missing English remains explicitly pending. Language switches call neither a supplier nor the model.
