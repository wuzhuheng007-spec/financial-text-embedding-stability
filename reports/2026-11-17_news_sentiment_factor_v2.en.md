# News sentiment factor pilot v2: real text scoring, limited development check

Run date: 2026-11-17, Asia/Shanghai. This run used saved NVDA and MU official earnings exhibits dated within 2025-04-01–2026-03-31. These are previously seen training materials. Issuer-disclosure wording is neither a complete media-news archive nor a verified event outcome, return direction, or tradable signal. April–July 2026 return labels were not opened or rescored.

LM uses (positive-word count minus negative-word count) divided by English word-token count. FinBERT stores all three class scores and uses positive minus negative. F1 equally averages selected blocks within an event. F2 subtracts the mean F1 of the issuer's preceding two same-type events; insufficient history remains missing. The 163 block scores, eight event rows, and event-level price alignment stay in private local artifacts. Public receipts contain hashes and aggregate counts.

Under the fixed descriptive procedure, pooled Pearson correlations between F1 and one-day open-to-open return were −0.555 for LM and −0.491 for FinBERT (eight events each). Five-day values were −0.036 and +0.073 (six events each). F2 has at most four one-day and three five-day observations, below the preregistered minimum of six, so no F2 correlation was reported. The tiny reused set and unstable date-block intervals do not support significance, causality, profitability, or generalization. The factor direction was not reversed after seeing outcomes. **No A/B/C/D fit ran, so incremental text value relative to price or announcement count is unknown.**

Strict historical first availability is not established. The conservative date proxy waits one complete regular U.S. trading session after the announcement date, enters at the following open, and exits at the open one or five trading days later. It uses saved unadjusted opens and excludes a label if a registered split or dividend date falls within its interval. This does not prove contemporary content availability or executable trading. The observed March 2026 LM dictionary edition was not proven available at each 2025 signal date. No meaningful cross-sectional IC was computed for two stocks.
