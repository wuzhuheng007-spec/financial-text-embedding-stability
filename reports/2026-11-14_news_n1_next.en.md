## What this establishes

The existing source-bound file-prefix sample is reproducibly readable in this workspace. The v2 sampler now distinguishes true EOF from a bounded byte prefix on the tested synthetic cases. The fixed output contract can mechanically reject schema, binding and Unicode `[start,end)` body-slice errors. These are software and provenance findings. The evidence spans use **original `Article` Unicode code-point indices**, with the body hash and no normalization mapping; a valid span or schema says nothing by itself about whether a company-event interpretation is correct.

The saved 100 records are a file prefix with one candidate ticker. The first 10 were already used for field design and cannot serve as an independent accuracy test. The private human pack has blank human fields; its separate AI drafts are **not** human gold labels. The model gate is a targeted cache/runtime search, not proof that no generative model exists anywhere on the machine. With zero completed calls and zero spans checked, no JSON-validity rate, extraction accuracy, F1, agreement, or financial efficacy can be calculated from this round.

## Remaining needs

For a real bounded smoke, supply an **existing offline, version-bound generative snapshot** with complete weights, tokenizer and chat template, plus a compatible local inference runtime; the runner must verify hashes and full-context fit before any call. Separately, a human reviewer should fill the first-pass adjudication fields before opening the AI draft reference, then record rule changes and disagreements. That batch remains development material. A later quality study needs an independent sample and grouping of duplicate or shared events. Historical article availability, company–security identity and the accepted financial protocol remain prerequisites for financial evaluation. New news-model inference, extraction-quality evaluation and financial experiments are **NOT_RUN**.
