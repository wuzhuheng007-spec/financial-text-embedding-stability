## Source and definition findings

The current July 2026 [CRSP CIZ guide, printed pp. 14–15](https://indexes.morningstar.com/api/docs/6a71c517e35d02a71403136d) distinguishes security and issuer history rows and defines type, class, listing-exchange and status fields. Its downloaded PDF has SHA-256 `e42f452207d4a30ef05de542a2dac9522f240100cec99a0309b1b3ab20699ec6`; the saved WRDS extract's release remains `UNKNOWN`. The [ShareCode crosswalk](https://www.crsp.org/wp-content/uploads/ShareCode.html) demonstrates combinations of security, share and issuer codes; it does not choose M5 eligibility. `PrimaryExch` identifies a security's primary listing exchange, not which of an issuer's classes is the primary issue or its event-time MIC. `USIncFlg` concerns incorporation rather than listing venue. The WRDS Historical Identifiers page cited in the incoming packet redirected to a maintenance page during this review and was not used to assert source-field semantics.

The [CIZ guide, printed pp. 17–18 and 31–33](https://indexes.morningstar.com/api/docs/6a71c517e35d02a71403136d) distinguishes traded close from price that may use a bid/ask average, price-type and return-missing flags, factors affecting adjusted price, and the daily delisting-return flag. The July 2026 [metadata guide, printed p. 9](https://indexes.morningstar.com/api/docs/6a454f87099ffc5dc9efb9cb), downloaded PDF SHA-256 `1a684df9d3f5a0692625cbf7319676d825d8e74dc2e6ab66ebf8d7aca4643fd6`, explains the CIZ price/price-flag split. A literal blank, a vendor missing code, a nonfinite number, a missing fixed-session row and a financial exclusion are different conditions. No label calculation follows from the field-presence counts.

The accepted code-2/3 screen is a review queue, not `accepted=true`. A dated attribute does not solve CIK link validity, and a candidate issue does not supply a formal sample. If a narrower complete-case design is proposed, its changed population must be declared before outcomes are inspected. No choice is silently inferred from apparent coverage or field labels.

**Verification scope:** The new attribute `verify-output` reparsed referenced rows from the bound original security CSVs and reaggregated the saved issuer index projection. It rehashed and header-checked the original issuer CSVs but did **not** reparse all their rows; it did not read the eight raw Daily CSVs. The vendor extract's release, complete field codebook, historical CIK validity, primary issue, event-time MIC, price semantics and formal event mask remain open. The source-attribute inventory is completed engineering work, not M5 cohort admission.
