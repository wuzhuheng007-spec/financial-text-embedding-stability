## Goal and result

The goal was to make the user's technology and ETF observation pool explicit and connect an independently running, read-only quote push. **A bounded one-symbol WebSocket connection succeeded while the US market was closed; market-open updates and sustained collection remain unverified.** The earlier WebAPI grant with `quote:write` and `trade:read` was rejected and discarded. After the user approved a broader consent flow, the later token actually granted only `quote:read` plus a redacted account selector; it was encrypted locally with DPAPI. A single quota GET returned HTTP 404, leaving the account limit unknown. The original adapter failed to recognize the server's idless `ret_code` control envelope; after a structural diagnostic and fix, two bounded TQQQ runs authenticated and received subscribe acknowledgments. One unique quote was persisted, but its provider timestamp was from Friday, its delay status was unknown, and the Sunday US market-state code was `11` ([official enum: `AFTER_HOURS_END`](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/naming-dictionary)). No open-market update, completed one-minute bar, new prediction or market outcome was obtained. The existing Codex MCP `quote:read` credential was not exported to WebAPI.
