## What the bounded sample shows

The [aggregate profile](../results/news_n1_20261114/sample_profile.json) covers exactly the 100 scanned and saved records. All 100 have nonempty title, candidate ticker, URL and `Article` fields; `Publisher` and `Author` are empty in these records. The `Article` values contain multiline prose and are separate from four named summary columns; there is no sentiment-label column in the observed header. The 100 URLs are distinct; there are 90 distinct exact titles and 96 distinct exact body hashes. The sample contains one distinct candidate ticker and URLs under one domain. These are sample descriptions, not estimates of full-dataset coverage or data quality.

All 100 saved `Date` strings have seconds and a literal `UTC`; 99 are exactly midnight. Their sampled calendar dates span 2023-05-24 to 2023-12-16. A stored UTC string is not evidence of the original publisher's time precision or first market availability. The source ticker is only a candidate company association. No historical company–security link was admitted.

In the [first-10 field check](../results/news_n1_20261114/field_review_receipt.json), 11 draft dispositions were recorded: eight company-event records, one article without a supported company link, and two without an identifiable event. One sampled article's stored date conflicted with a date in its prose; another carried a source ticker without naming that company in its body. Market-outcome-style articles also appear. These cases justify separate timing, entity-link and event-versus-outcome fields; they do not quantify general extraction accuracy. An initial private draft used a text-kind label that could imply complete publisher text; a preserved second draft corrects it to `ARTICLE_FIELD_PROSE_COMPLETENESS_UNKNOWN`.

The reusable [bounded sampler](../scripts/sample_fnspid_news.py) and [aggregate profiler](../scripts/profile_fnspid_sample.py) are hardened v2 successors to the exact ignored local v1 scripts used for this run; they are not byte-identical. The executed v1 script hashes remain in the source receipt and local manifest. The published v2 sampler was not used to redownload the real sample. [Code validation](../results/news_n1_20261114/code_validation.log) records seven focused synthetic tests passing, including quoted multiline CSV, sentinels, range limits and partial quoted records, plus Python compilation.
