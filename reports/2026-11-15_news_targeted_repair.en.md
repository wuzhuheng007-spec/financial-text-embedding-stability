## What the saved failures show

The five Schema error groups in the [offline diagnostic](../results/news_targeted_repair_20261115/wire_failure_diagnostic.json) are `/article_disposition` (4 enum instances), `/article_evidence/*/role` (4 enum), `/records/*/evidence_spans/*/role` (4 enum), `/market_outcome_direction` (1 const), and `/records/*/unknown_reason` (1 not). These are **error instances**, not 14 articles. All 12 enum instances were categorized as unlisted strings; the specific private values are withheld. The two other failures are cross-field constraints. The targeted prompt explicitly lists allowed labels, links market outcome state to direction, links unknown impact to reason, and asks for short verbatim body quotes. `event_type` remains free text. The one missing quote still blocks canonical construction in the old response; it has not been silently repaired.

The old [v4 aggregate](../results/news_grounding_runtime_20261114/inference_aggregate.json) recorded a single JSON-invalid response and a token-budget truncation clue. It does not prove the cause of invalid JSON. For ordinal 2, the [native interruption recheck](../results/news_targeted_repair_20261115/native_interruption_recheck.json) found a committed launch and a worker-created CUDA snapshot, with no saved stderr, exit code, model-load marker, generation-start marker or response. Windows recorded contemporaneous `python.exe`/`c10.dll` (`0xc0000005`) and `codex.exe` (`0xc0000409`) application errors; their causal relationship is **UNKNOWN**. No OOM was recorded in the v4 run; that observation cannot be turned into a GPU or attention-backend explanation. The new stop rule changes run lifecycle relative to the old recovery and must be considered when comparing coverage.

The [v5 configuration](../configs/news_n1_smoke_v5_targeted.json) binds the original-author FNSPID revision and sample hash, the pinned Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct revision, FP16, SDPA request, seed 1729, wire/canonical contracts, and limits of ten calls, 1,024 new tokens, 180 seconds per call, 1,800 seconds total and zero retries. Source and artifact hashes are in the config and receipts. The ten articles are reused development material; exact string matching, host-computed offsets and mechanical validity do not establish correct affected companies, events or impact directions.

## Results and next gate

The [independent v5 audit](../results/news_targeted_repair_20261115/inference_aggregate.json) passed. The run took 03:51:56–04:11:13 local time. Its stage sequence is **10/10** fixed articles attempted and generation-started; **7/10** responses saved; **6/7** saved responses JSON-valid; **0/6** JSON-valid responses wire-valid. The other positions were **three `CALL_TIMEOUT`**, **one `JSON_PARSE_ERROR`** (at the 1,024-token output cap, a truncation clue rather than a proven cause), and **six `WIRE_SCHEMA_INVALID`**. The six wire-invalid responses yielded **nine Schema error instances**: four `market_outcome_direction` const, two `records[].evidence_spans` minItems, two `records[].unknown_reason` enum and one `records` maxItems. Error instances are not article counts. No quote was checked because no response passed wire validation; quote success is **NOT_EVALUATED (0/0 quotes)**. Canonical construction is **0/0 conditional wire-valid responses, NOT_EVALUATED**; no complete company-event output was obtained. This is a mechanical outcome, not a semantic accuracy estimate.

The [paired comparison](../results/news_targeted_repair_20261115/paired_comparison.json) binds the same sample, model revision, wire/canonical Schemas, adapter and validator. Both v4 and v5 attempted the ten positions; v4 saved nine responses with one wire-valid but quote-unresolved response, while v5 saved seven with none wire-valid. Only **six** positions have saved responses in both runs, and wire-valid counts on that paired subset are **0/6** in each version. Missing positions remain in the transition table. The targeted prompt is 644 tokens longer for each fixed input; the new abnormal-exit policy also differs. The aggregate error-instance counts and overall wire coverage have different response denominators, so they do not establish that any field error improved. Three v5 timeouts occurred without retry; no OOM or native crash was recorded. The actual SDPA kernel is still **UNKNOWN**. Neither runtime differences nor the prompt can be assigned a causal effect from this single reused development set.
