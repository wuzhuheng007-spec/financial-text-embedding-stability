## Price timing and adjustment audit

The old target is session *t* open-to-close direction. Its six price/volume inputs end at *t−2*, leaving all of *t−1* unused. The original task permitted a decision before the *t−1* close, when its completed daily bar would not yet be available. This comparison fixes a hypothetical decision at 09:30 America/New_York on *t−1* and retains the same B features. It is not a prediction recorded at that historical time. A later design using *t−1* close must move the decision after that bar is complete and available, in a separate version.

The [row audit](../results/market_observer_historical_official_20261115/price_audit_redacted.json) recomputed the dates, features and labels for 939 saved rows. Adjusted and raw returns differ materially across registered actions: TQQQ's split creates an approximately 0.693 mechanical jump in raw cross-day log return. [ProShares](https://www.proshares.com/press-releases/proshares-announces-etf-share-splits5) confirms a 2:1 TQQQ split effective before the 2025-11-20 market open. Rebuilding from raw bars and only actions dated through each feature session matched the frozen features within about `1.03e-9`; actions after the window multiply both endpoints of an in-window log ratio and cancel. This supports keeping B for a paired retrospective comparison. Provider factor semantics and historical bar availability are still not independently established. The [moomoo historical K-line documentation](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/basic-data/history-kline) defines `autype=0/1` as unadjusted/forward-adjusted.

There were no 4xx or 429 responses. The [monthly table](../results/market_observer_official_20261115/corpus_coverage_redacted.json) separates planned, full body, failed, duplicate and unknown counts. Raw text remains local.

Five SEC index rows have a `filingDate` one day later than the acceptance timestamp converted to Eastern time. Both fields are retained; the conservative proxy date *D* takes the later. After *D*, the first **full US regular session strictly later than D** is *F*. Text becomes eligible no earlier than the following session's open. The real trading calendar and the hypothetical decision use America/New_York time. This prevents an after-hours filing from moving to an earlier decision window, but does **not** prove when the body was first visible. Independently verified strict first-availability records remain **zero**. An accession URL and today's body hash support only the retrospective proxy. The private manifest separates publisher, acceptance, release date, current retrieval, version status and event group; the public index contains source locations and redacted status.

## Reused validation development comparison

C uses only SEC primary filings and same-accession earnings exhibits. It excludes Qwen candidate financial figures, Jin10 search hits, mutable IR and FOMC pages, and later development cards. Fixed cleaning and length limits aggregate date-eligible events over the latest 20 sessions. Zero announcements are assigned only where that designated SEC source's monthly coverage is complete; unknown coverage is missing. Vocabulary, IDF, scaling and model parameters are fitted on training rows only. A is training-majority direction; B is the prior six-feature price/volume logistic model; C adds TF-IDF, announcement count and a coverage flag. B and C use the old `{0.1, 1, 10}` L2 grid on the same training and validation rows, retaining every candidate. Their coefficients are refitted on that common sample. The [config](../configs/market_observer_official_text_v1.json) and [receipt](../results/market_observer_official_20261115/text_comparison_redacted.json) bind source versions and private row-output hashes.

The [comparison receipt](../results/market_observer_official_20261115/text_comparison_redacted.json) lists the exact dates and reasons. NVDA C exceeds B balanced accuracy by about 10.1 percentage points but remains below A; MU shows no difference. C adds both TF-IDF and announcement count, so the B-to-C difference cannot be attributed to text alone. This one-source, two-security, reused-validation exercise does **not** establish a stable announcement increment. Character caps clipped 18 event documents and three eligible rows' aggregate text. The previously exposed June–July 2026 test interval was not scored or used for selection. There is no profit, causal, formal M5 or independent prediction result.
