This round checks separately: (1) fragment presence; (2) reachability of model-visible shared references; (3) exact binding of numeric cell to row, column, period, unit and accounting basis; and (4) same-team review of source financial meaning. ## Core questions and negative findings

1. **Are periods and units bound to the correct value columns?** No fully source-checked case passed. For six mechanically reachable NVDA candidates, the value cells and row metrics were correctly located, but **all 12 column-header links point to other numeric data rows in the same columns**. The real period/unit header was parsed as data. Its source period, table unit and GAAP basis did not reach these inputs correctly. See the [structural root-cause receipt](../results/market_observer_financial_text_v23_20261116/postrun_false_header_diagnosis_redacted.json).
2. **Does a header or accounting basis still inherit from the wrong scope?** All six witnesses received false column headers; a section-level billions unit was carried in while the table declared millions. The true GAAP header was absent, so reachable but wrong context cannot establish accounting basis. Some cross-subtable conflicts are rejected in synthetic tests, yet real row classification still failed. This is same-team readback of six candidates in three documents, not a conclusion about all filings.
3. **What parsed evidence is incomplete in the serialized input?** Among 60 supported candidates, 42 had a unique numeric cell but no selected group: 16 from relation conflict and 26 unresolved relations. Eight more have ambiguous cell matches, four are paragraphs without evaluable table-grid links, and six selected cells have semantically wrong context. The **24 whole-group budget omissions** in the [build receipt](../results/market_observer_financial_text_v23_20261116/build_frozen_r2_redacted.json) count all evidence groups, not 24 reviewed facts; none of the diagnosed 42 candidate omissions was attributed to budget.
4. **Did v2.3 regress on old-complete entries?** All 34 previously fragment-complete candidates are incomplete under v2.3's literal check. The [regression interpretation](../results/market_observer_financial_text_v23_20261116/postrun_interpretation_frozen_r2_evalfix1_redacted.json) separates two conflict omissions, 18 unresolved omissions, eight ambiguous cell matches and six selected cells with split-fragment mismatch and wrong semantic context. This is a strict literal-metric regression with a real context error; 0/71 alone cannot establish that all financial information is absent.
5. **Which items remain candidates?** All six mechanically reachable witnesses failed same-team source review, for **0/6 fully confirmed**. Unresolved/conflicting relations, ambiguous cell matches and no-grid paragraphs remain candidates or unevaluable. The single next rule candidate is a strict semantic period/unit table-header classifier before topic/numeric data-row logic, with currency alone forbidden from promoting a data row to a header. This [post-run root-cause proposal](../results/market_observer_financial_text_v23_20261116/postrun_false_header_diagnosis_redacted.json) was **not run**; the frozen v2.3 result was not retuned.
