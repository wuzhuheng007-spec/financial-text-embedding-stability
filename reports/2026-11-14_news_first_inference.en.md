## What the run can establish

The bounded run met its engineering success condition of saving and reading back actual model responses. JSON parse validity was **9/9 completed responses** and **9/10 attempted articles**. Schema validity was **0/9 parseable responses** and **0/10 attempts**. The conditional denominator for binding and full mechanical validity was zero, so those conditional rates are **NOT_EVALUATED**; both have 0/10 on the attempted-article denominator. One call timed out, while the nine saved responses ended at EOS. The aggregate [audit receipt](../results/news_first_inference_20261114/inference_aggregate.json) binds these counts to input, model, config, prompt, schema and runner hashes. The schema failures are real failure outcomes; no prompt or schema was revised during this run. The raw source article fields, exact prompts, generated text and token IDs, parsed row-level outputs and errors remain in ignored local artifacts. The public receipt provides counts and denominator definitions without reproducing article text or model answers. A JSON parse, schema check or exact Unicode evidence slice is **mechanical validation**, not proof that the model identified the right event, target company or business impact.

These ten source-order articles were already used for field design. They are a development set from a bounded file prefix with one candidate ticker, not an independent test sample. The private human-review fields remain blank. No extraction accuracy, F1, human agreement or calibrated confidence can be estimated. Original publication instants and historical company–security identity remain unverified for finance. The exact model training cutoff is not established by its revision; running it retrospectively on 2023 text does not demonstrate no-lookahead prediction.

## Next work

Review the saved private responses and failure codes against the frozen schema and prompt. If changes are warranted, create a new version rather than silently rewriting this run. Complete independent human adjudication on the development material, then use a separate sample and event-cluster grouping for extraction-quality evaluation. Historical news availability, company–security identity, price/calendar rules and an accepted financial protocol remain separate prerequisites. Financial labels, predictions, returns and trading evaluation are **NOT_RUN**.
