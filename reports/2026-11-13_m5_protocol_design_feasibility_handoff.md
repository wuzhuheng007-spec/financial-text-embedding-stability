## Time, market, price and missingness gates

The SEC describes `FILED AS OF DATE` as the assigned official filing date, which may change after a post-acceptance correction. It separately describes acceptance time and states that sec.gov has **no timestamp recording the instant filing content first became available**. SEC filing-date guidance explains that after-hours acceptance can lead to a next-business-day filing date or dissemination. Therefore the first exchange session strictly after the **official filing date** is an analytic location, not a witnessed first-public time. Bind accession, fiscal period, amendments/corrections and extracted Item 1 bytes before any event is included. A later fixed model supports a retrospective fixed-snapshot comparison, not strict historical model availability. [SEC FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions), [filing-date guidance](https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/filer-support-resources/how-do-i-guides/determine-status-my-filing), [EDGAR data guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data).

The current [CIZ guide, printed pp. 17–18](https://indexes.morningstar.com/api/docs/6a71c517e35d02a71403136d) defines `DlyClose` as the last regular-session trade, while `DlyPrc` may use a bid/ask average when no trade price exists. `DlyFacPrc` covers factors affecting price adjustment in return calculations, and `DlyRetx` describes a return without ordinary dividends. These definitions do not certify the saved WRDS extract's release or a split-only accumulated factor. Neither design substitutes `DlyPrc` for `DlyClose`, adopts `DlyRetx` as the target, or constructs a label until adjustment, nonordinary actions and terminal outcomes are source-backed and accepted. The accepted Daily representative-row rule does not resolve a missing fixed-session price.

The original proposed `d+h` endpoint remains fixed by the applicable independent exchange calendar for `h=5` or `h=21`. The proposed feature lookback requires 21 closes including `d`; the full `d` through `d+h` future window requires finite positive prices at **every** required session, rather than just two endpoints. Missing bars never move an endpoint or become zero. The XNYS baseline is retrospective and does not certify another issue's market. A future 60-predecision-session dollar-volume primary-class alternative is not accepted; the November–December 2019 buffer alone does not guarantee 60 prior sessions for early-2020 decisions. Do not use that alternative simply to fill missing primary evidence.
