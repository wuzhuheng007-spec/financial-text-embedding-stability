## 3. Numerical candidate and joining unit

The round-8 proposal keeps the round-7 one-original-filing event, retrospective interpretation, 2020–2023/2024/2025 chronological train/validation/test years, five-session primary and 21-session alternative, F0 price-only/F1 lexical/F2 fixed 128/equal dense arms, 32 text coordinates, three Ridge alphas, common sample, label purging and held-out score. It adds exact transformations in the [machine-readable candidate](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round8.json): lowercase `[a-z][a-z0-9]+` tokens, train-only top-5,000 document-frequency vocabulary with lexical ties, raw counts, `log((1+N)/(1+df))+1` IDF, row L2 normalization, deterministic **uncentered** float64 full SVD for lexical text and **centered, unscaled** float64 full SVD for dense PCA. Numerical rank must support 32 components in both training and allowed refit windows. Empty tokenized sections fail the shared row; all-OOV future sections are flagged and yield zero lexical coordinates; nonfinite and zero-fit cases fail rather than acquire imputed values. No stochastic SVD or training seed is proposed.

The train-window scaler uses float64, population standard deviation (`ddof=0`) and logged zero-variance coordinate removal. Ridge minimizes a **sum** of squared errors plus `alpha*sum(beta^2)` on scaled features with an unpenalized intercept. The proposed deterministic solver is float64 `numpy.linalg.lstsq(rcond=None)` on a `sqrt(alpha)`-augmented design. No test rows fit vocabulary, IDF, SVD, PCA, scaler, alpha or predictor; final refit uses only eligible train+validation rows whose labels are available before the first test decision. Undefined reference SSE, constant/too-small monthly rank score, nonfinite inputs or a missing arm produce named null/failure states, never a complete three-arm score. These are proposed *method rules*, not executed calculations.

A filing CIK must be joined to one historically valid **security**, not a current ticker string. The recommended rule requires a stable issue ID, effective-dated CIK link, common-share type and unique historical primary listing at the decision time, with ticker changes and exits retained. A genuine unresolved multi-class mapping becomes `AMBIGUOUS_SECURITY` under the common three-arm mask. A separately accepted alternative could rank classes by predecision 60-session dollar volume; choosing a class by future price/label completeness is disallowed. The five development CIKs remain excluded from a future formal queue. A tiny substitute cohort cannot automatically retain 32 dimensions: centered PCA needs at least 33 eligible training rows and the actual numerical rank may still be lower.
