The 27 pre-run tests overlap with the 33 final tests; they are not 60 independent tests. Neither test log measures extraction accuracy.

## Retest outcome and limits

The old run attempted **10/10**, saved **9** responses and had **0/9 Schema-valid** completed responses. The v3 run attempted **3/10**, saved **2** responses and had **2/2 Schema-valid** completed responses (**2/3 attempted; 2/10 fixed target**). This describes format compliance on two reused development articles, not a general improvement rate. Both v3 outputs remained **mechanically invalid (0/2)**: one had `ARTICLE_REASON_INCONSISTENT`, one had `SPAN_SLICE_MISMATCH`; **0/3 checked evidence spans** matched the original body exactly. The schema-valid dispositions were one `COMPANY_EVENTS_FOUND` with one record and one `NO_SUPPORTED_COMPANY` with zero records. Strict synthetic-example copy indicators were **0/2**; these indicators do not test all forms of copying or semantic accuracy.

The paired status table covers all ten fixed article positions: **2** old `SCHEMA_INVALID` → new `MECHANICAL_INVALID`; **6** old `SCHEMA_INVALID` → new `NOT_ATTEMPTED_AFTER_OOM_STOP`; **1** old `SCHEMA_INVALID` → new `OOM_STOP`; **1** old `CALL_TIMEOUT` → new `NOT_ATTEMPTED_AFTER_OOM_STOP`. The change in prompt added **1,831 tokens** to each input and combined full Schema text with the invented example. With two completed v3 responses and an OOM stop, there is no stable throughput or causal prompt-effect estimate. The public comparison contains aggregate transitions only; row-level text and pair details stay local.

The first ten articles were already used for field and prompt design. Even a mechanical improvement on this paired development set cannot establish general extraction quality or isolate the Schema text from the synthetic example. The private human-adjudication fields remain blank. A JSON-valid, schema-valid or exact evidence-span result would still require independent human review to judge event, company and direction correctness. Historical article availability, company–security identity and the accepted financial protocol remain unresolved; financial labels, prediction and return evaluation are **NOT_RUN**.
