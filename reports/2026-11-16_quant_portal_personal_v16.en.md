The supplier has not established an official overnight trade-date rule or a per-quote latency flag in the checked fields. These remain explicit evidence gaps, consistent with the [official market-state](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/basic-data/market-state) and [stock-quote](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/realtime/stock-quote) field checks.

Jin10 is shown as the news supplier. Where the supplier did not provide a headline, the first complete source sentence is visibly labeled an excerpt. Of the 47 items in the audited display window, 45 used that fallback, one used an incomplete excerpt, and one had a supplier headline. A reported media attribution is not treated as independent verification of the original publisher.

A prior failure was requeued under the new contract; repeat enqueue produced zero new jobs. The current contract is `personal_news_conditional_v16_4`.
