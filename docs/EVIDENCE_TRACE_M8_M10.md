## Interpretation and remaining gates

The executed M10 result is a **two-document, fixed-model, agent-authored retrieval engineering check**. It is neither a five-company quarterly representation study nor a formal M5 experiment. GOOGL's two sparse-header associations are unresolved; the M10 guard prevents their use as verified numeric header relationships. Q6 does not establish semantic abstention, and a retrieval-only response is not a generated answer. No general retrieval-quality claim, financial incremental-value claim, historical trading decision time, or freedom from look-ahead bias follows. Formal M5 sample, time split, model comparison, endpoints, and acceptance metrics remain to be decided. All source and output readbacks cited here are same-agent self-checks; independent human assessment would require a separate reviewer and record.
