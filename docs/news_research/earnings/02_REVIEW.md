# Review boundaries / 审核边界
- 本轮为范围和任务更新；六家公司数量是用户指定范围，不是六份财报已采集。This update specifies scope; it does not claim six earnings reports have been collected.
- 官方入口已在线打开；部分动态页面只返回目录/框架。具体数字、原件哈希、公告时刻与共识仍需下一轮实采。Entry pages were opened; dynamic pages may expose only an index. Numbers, hashes, release instants and consensus still require collection.
- 新闻截图未经逐条核验，不转存为事实。Screenshots are unverified examples.
- 同一发布事件下的快讯聚类；价格变化不得泄漏至发布前预测。Cluster flash items; isolate later price observations.
- 没有披露不等于0；未取得共识不等于没有市场预期。Missing disclosure is not zero; unavailable consensus does not mean no expectations existed.
- 人工偏好、人工事实标准、结构格式校验与抽取准确性是四件事。Preference, gold labels, format validation and extraction accuracy are distinct.
- 历史12候选、旧模型错误及M5身份阻塞保持原状态。Preserve historical candidates, model failures and M5 blockers.
- 计划—执行表记录未完成及原因，单一开发集不能支持普遍结论。Report incomplete work and reasons; no generalization from one development set.
