## 2. Required earnings-card sections
Performance: revenue, operating income, net income, diluted EPS, gross/operating margins and comparable YoY/QoQ changes.
Guidance: next-quarter/full-year revenue, margins, EPS and CapEx where disclosed; retain ranges and previous guidance.
Cash: operating cash flow, CapEx, free cash flow and cash equivalents.
Financing: debt, interest expense, maturities and financing terms. Coupon, yield and all-in financing cost are different measures.
Business: reported segment revenue, growth, orders/backlog/RPO, concentration and inventory.
Expectations: actual versus prerelease consensus; new guidance versus consensus for the same future period.
Interpretation: management statements, source locations, verified facts, conditional transmission hypotheses and observable checks.

Each number requires issuer, period start/end, fiscal label, quarterly/YTD basis, currency/unit/scale, consolidated/segment scope, accounting and adjustment basis, source URL/locator/hash, release and first-availability times with precision, retrieval time and version. Preserve raw and normalized values.
Separate consolidated, attributable and adjusted earnings; basic/diluted EPS; quarterly and cumulative cash flows. Derive quarters from cumulative values only on matching bases with both inputs recorded. Cash PP&E purchases, asset additions, finance leases and issuer-defined CapEx must not be silently combined. Preserve reported FCF definitions; label calculated OCF-minus-cash-CapEx separately. Missing disclosure is NOT_DISCLOSED; unsuccessful search is NOT_FOUND, never zero.

## 4. Expectations, timing and authenticity
Store first-release macro values, previously known values and newly revised values separately. Consensus requires a prerelease provider snapshot, timestamp and coverage. An official actual is not an analyst consensus. Without valid consensus, report growth or changes from previous guidance without claiming a beat.
Raw surprise equals actual minus consensus. Relative surprise uses absolute consensus as denominator only when nonzero and comparable. Treat loss-to-profit and zero bases separately; express margin/rate differences in percentage points or basis points. Guidance-midpoint comparison is a display statistic, not a replacement for its range.

Screenshots establish topic/layout preferences only; their allegations, price moves and financial values were not individually verified in this update. Label them UNVERIFIED and exclude them from facts/labels. Jin10/moomoo can support discovery; seek issuer IR, regulatory filings or original agencies and preserve the reporting chain. Access and storage rights are separate.
Multiple flash items from one report form one event with individual arrival times. After-hours returns are outcomes, not prerelease inputs. Isolate returns embedded in market summaries from forecasting features. Preserve local timezone and UTC; do not infer exact publication instants from dates.

## 5. Transmission to technology firms
Show facts, new information relative to expectations, possible rate/demand/cost/credit channels, issuer exposure, conditions/counterevidence and subsequent observations. Every edge needs evidence or AI_ANALYST_DRAFT status, not a causal claim.
A cloud CapEx increase could support supplier orders and pressure the buyer's near-term cash flow, conditional on investment mix, timing, funding and prior expectations. Memory price changes can affect producers and buyers differently depending on contracts, inventory and pass-through. Store narrative sentiment, fundamental impact and observed market response separately.
