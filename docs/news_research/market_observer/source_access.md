## Standalone WebAPI push authorization result

The official [OpenAPI OAuth guide](https://open.moomoo.com/api/overview/getting-started) distinguishes registration capabilities from the scopes actually granted in a token response; [WebSocket quote authentication](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/push/auth) accepts a Bearer token. An earlier completed token exchange returned `quote:write` and `trade:read` beyond the requested `quote:read`; the application rejected and discarded it. Client registration also advertised broader capabilities, which alone do not prove a granted token. See the [earlier redacted authorization receipt](../../../results/watchlist_live_20261115/authorization_failure_redacted.json). In a later user-approved flow, the actual grant was limited to `quote:read` plus a redacted account selector; DPAPI protects that credential locally. The Codex MCP token was not reused. One account quota GET returned HTTP 404, leaving quota UNKNOWN.

The [redacted live-probe receipt](../../../results/watchlist_live_20261115/live_probe_redacted.json) distinguishes the first failed control-frame interpretation from later successful bounded sessions. The service sent no trade or account-position calls. Moomoo returned idless `ret_code` control envelopes, rather than the documented example's request-ID shape; a structural diagnostic observed successful authentication, `MARKET_STATE` and `QUOTE` frames. After adapter repair, two one-key `US.TQQQ` runs authenticated and acknowledged the subscription. The final run reported `CONNECTED_MARKET_CLOSED` with US state code 11 ([official enum: `AFTER_HOURS_END`](https://open.moomoo.com/api/quote/naming-dictionary)); the unique saved quote carried Friday provider time and UNKNOWN delay. The final unsubscribe was acknowledged and all sessions stopped. This verifies a closed-market connection and local persistence, not open-market real-time updating, a 19-key entitlement or long-term service stability.
