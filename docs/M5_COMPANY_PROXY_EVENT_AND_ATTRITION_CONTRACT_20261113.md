# M5 company event and attrition contract: preparation draft

**Status:** `DRAFT_NOT_ACCEPTED_SCHEMA_ONLY / ENGINEERING_ONLY`. The [machine schema](../configs/m5_company_proxy_event_attrition_schema_20261113.json) and [empty template](../configs/m5_company_proxy_event_attrition_template_20261113.json) specify evidence fields for a proposed company filing-event comparison with a retrospective session proxy. They contain no real event, company, security, label, score or admitted-sample count. They do not select the formal 2020 cohort or authorize financial execution. The existing [round-8 proposal](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round8.json) and [H review option](../configs/m5_protocol_design_h_20261113.json) remain separate historical bytes.

## Unit, denominators and time axis

The proposed unit is **one original company 10-K Item 1 filing event** identified by an authoritative company CIK, accession, fiscal period and official SEC Filing Date. An accession prefix alone is not a company-CIK source. Preserve the original form, later corrections and amendments as distinct source records; do not replace an original event with a later filing or text extraction. The fixed text requires source-document and Item 1 byte SHA-256 values, extraction code/configuration hashes, source vintage and actual extraction time. A later fixed extraction is suitable only for the declared retrospective interpretation. Neither SEC acceptance time nor Filing Date proves the first-public instant or the exact text's historic availability.

The **starting-company denominator** would be the number of companies selected under a separately accepted 2020 source, eligibility, exclusion and deterministic ordering rule, before filing-event, identity or price exclusions. The 7,639 starting securities in the saved engineering intake are not this number. The **potential-event denominator** would enumerate original filings for that starting company set and the proposed decision years before security-link, text-version, calendar or price-window admission. Both denominators are currently `null`; the five development filings cannot populate them. No formal admitted-event denominator exists. Record all missing, duplicate, amended and ambiguous candidates so that a later mask can be reconciled without silently changing its source population.

For each potential event, derive the proxy session `d` only after the applicable market and versioned calendar are supported: the first regular session whose date is strictly after the official SEC Filing Date. This is an analytic position, with **no inferred intraday 18:00 cutoff**, historic public-time claim, finalized-close availability claim or executable trade claim. Store the fixed `d+5` and `d+21` session positions separately. A missing session price never shifts either endpoint. Source and definition of any filing-date correction must be retained.

The [saved H/R feasibility report](../reports/2026-11-13_m5_protocol_design_feasibility_handoff.md) reports 2,535 / 2,533 strict fixed-snapshot structural CIK-security pairs under two proposed endpoint conventions. Those numbers are **not** event-time identities, primary issues, eligible companies or an admitted event count. Keep repeated observations of the same CIK-security pair separate from genuinely different candidate CIKs or securities. The source interval endpoint convention and the literal `EQTY/COM/NS/ACOR/CORP` diagnostic have not been accepted as eligibility rules.

## Horizon-specific masks and attrition

For each horizon `h` in `{5,21}`, the proposed price-feature and target window requires a finite positive, source-valid traded close at **every applicable session from `d-20` through `d+h` inclusive**, after a separately specified split/action policy. The five-session mask must not require 21 future sessions. F0, F1 and F2 must use identical events and labels **within the same horizon**; the two horizons may have different retained event sets. No price or financial label is computed by this contract. A canonical traded close, split direction and event-date handling, nonordinary distribution treatment, known terminal value and unknown exit policy are still unresolved in the separate price/exit contract.

An unscored event remains in the initial and potential-event denominators with every applicable reason. Do not zero-fill a missing bar, move a fixed endpoint, substitute a successor issue, infer a terminal value or select a company using future price completeness. Retain both (1) all overlapping reason counts and (2) one mutually exclusive first-failure count **only after its precedence rule is accepted**. The latter reconciles `potential events = scored events + unscored events` for a fully evaluated, consistently scoped year and horizon. The annual axis (official filing year versus proxy decision-session year) is deliberately `null` in the empty template; decide it before filling year-by-reason counts and ensure year-boundary events remain traceable. Company counts, event counts, horizon rows and independent text counts are different units.

The template's two overall `horizon_attrition` rows have `NOT_EVALUATED` status and `null` counts; `annual_attrition` is empty because no year axis or event list is established. The schema permits 2019–2026 annual-axis values so a filing near a year boundary and its next-session proxy are not silently discarded; those bounds are schema capacity, not admitted study years. A later populated diagnostic needs one row per selected year-axis, year and horizon, plus reconciliation to each horizon total; duplicate keys must be rejected by a future validator. Empty reason maps mean **no reasons have been assessed**, not zero failures. A source or policy becoming available may support a new populated diagnostic, but this schema-only file and the present design preparation do not admit events. If eventual complete-window analysis excludes missing or exited cases, its claim is a **conditional company-event association among fully observed windows**, not full-cohort return performance. Retain by-year attrition and the broader company/event denominators so the restriction is visible.

1. Bind the exact proposed configuration and every input/source version, then document the chosen cohort and year axis. Do not infer source completeness from a row count.
2. Check original filing identity, authoritative CIK, fiscal period, corrections and exact text bytes before considering a security association.
3. Validate dated CIK-security and primary-issue evidence, interval containment, overlap, multiple classes and event-market/calendar applicability. An unresolved field remains `null`; no candidate is promoted by unique graph degree alone.
4. Fix `d`, `d+5` and `d+21` on the supported calendar, retain every required `d-20 ... d+h` session, and apply the separately accepted price/action/exit definitions. No future price can choose a starting company or primary share.
5. Reconcile horizon-specific potential, scored and unscored event counts and both exclusive and overlapping failure reasons. Apply the identical per-horizon event mask to F0/F1/F2. Keep formal admitted count `null` until the exact full protocol receives separate researcher acceptance and all source gates pass.
