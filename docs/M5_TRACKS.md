# M5 research tracks — decision draft, 19 October 2026

No formal experiment is authorized or run. The five-company M0–M4 outputs remain an engineering demonstration. The [corrected chronology](../reports/2026-10-19_text_price_time_alignment.md) verifies historical filing identities but does not certify exact first-public timestamps or point-in-time text vintage.

| Track | Necessary inputs | Acceptance before formal execution | Time requirement | Conclusion allowed if tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Recommended primary: fixed-text representation stability** | A predeclared multi-company, multi-filing corpus of frozen original Item 1 bytes and hashes; exact model/tokenizer revisions; chunk maps; lexical/metadata controls; planned perturbations and failure ledger. | Professor approves universe, section, model arms, estimand, primary similarity/Top-k metrics, uncertainty unit and thresholds. Re-run on identical bytes and verify model/input/output provenance. A single model or five-company pilot can only be a limited sensitivity result. | For a descriptive fixed-text comparison, freeze each document identity before comparing representations; no 2025 price pairing or trading decision time is required. Any claim about historical model availability or forward performance requires separate time evidence. | Stability or instability of the tested representations under the stated perturbations, within the selected corpus and models. No return-prediction, incremental-value or look-ahead-free financial claim. |
| **Optional extension: historical return prediction** | Timestamped filing/publication evidence or an approved conservative availability proxy; amendment/PAC and text-vintage policy; historical security identity; decision-time trailing features; post-decision labels; chronological train/test split; price-only, lexical and metadata baselines; dividend/corporate-action policy. | Approve task, horizon, lag sensitivity, universe, exclusions, label definition, fit boundaries, metrics and leakage tests before held-out evaluation. Fit TF-IDF, PCA and predictive models only within past training windows. **Availability proposal for the optional financial extensions:** use the first regular-session open strictly after the official SEC filing date, conditional on dissemination by the end of that date and no decision-time Item 1 correction. Compare one and two additional trading-session lags; use a same-day rule only if direct dissemination evidence is available. The rule and assumptions are **TBD for professor approval**. The saved 2026 download time is snapshot provenance and does not imply that a 2025 filing was unavailable in 2025. The saved 2026 text cannot yet be certified as identical to a historical decision-time version.

**2025 prices:** M4 used them as retrospective price-only PCA input. For a future protocol, the professor must choose whether any price data serve as trailing features, post-decision labels, or a static descriptive comparison. Each role implies a different date mask. `adjusted=true` bars are split-adjusted, not dividend total returns. The M4 PCA **K=2** is a demonstration setting with no recorded historical choice rationale; the formal K selection rule is **TBD** and is relevant only if PCA enters an approved track.
