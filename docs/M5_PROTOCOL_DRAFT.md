# M5 protocol: one-page decision sheet for researcher and professor

**DRAFT — no formal parameters frozen; no formal financial experiment authorized or run.** The recommendation is to make **representation stability the first primary task**. Future-return prediction or risk estimation may be a later, separately approved extension. The five-company M0–M4 work is an engineering demonstration only.

| Decision | Recommendation to discuss | Alternative | Reason | Data/evidence needed | Professor decision (all TBD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Primary task | Pre-register **fixed-text representation stability** of company similarities and Top-k peers under controlled text/model changes. | Historical return prediction or risk estimation only as separately gated extensions. | Directly addresses the representation question without assigning a financial outcome to the small 2025 price demo. | Frozen filing bytes and hashes, model provenance, pre-specified perturbations and firm-level stability outcomes; historical publication evidence only for a temporal/financial claim. | Primary estimand, hypothesis, and whether either financial extension is in scope. |
| 2. Universe and period | Choose a historical US equity universe by rules known at each date; start with original 10-K Item 1 and record every exclusion/amendment. | Fixed balanced company panel for a clearly labeled pilot. | Avoid survivor, ticker-history and missing-filing selection bias. | Historical security–CIK mapping, filings, reporting/acceptance/publication metadata and amendment history. | Universe, dates, section, eligibility and target sample size. |
| 3. Models and perturbations | Freeze at least two exact pretrained model/tokenizer revisions; vary one factor at a time, including chunk length, with reproducible inference. | One pinned model with several settings as a limited sensitivity study. | M2–M3 used one fixed-weight model; repetition is not an independent training seed. | Weight/tokenizer hashes, release/training-cutoff evidence, preprocessing, chunk maps, hardware and inference receipts. | Primary model(s), versions, perturbations, any training and genuinely independent seeds. |
| 4. Time and leakage | Freeze exact original text for the descriptive mainline; for a financial extension, use a decision time after documented public availability or an approved conservative proxy and lag. | Keep financial extensions unrun until their historical evidence gate passes. | [The corrected five-company chronology](../reports/2026-10-19_text_price_time_alignment.md) verifies 2025 filing identities. The 2026 download does not imply 2025 unavailability, but exact first-public time and unchanged historical text are unverified. The 2025 price-window role is unassigned. | For extensions: dissemination evidence or approved filing-date proxy, revision/PAC policy, timestamped feature lineage and chronological fit/evaluation boundaries. | Price role (trailing feature, future label, static comparison), lag/sensitivity, decision clock, time split and revision policy. |
| 5. Controls and financial extension | For stability, compare lexical TF-IDF and simple metadata/industry peers; if a financial extension is approved, add a fair price-only baseline and combined price+text model. | Stability-only analysis with no financial outcome. | The current TF-IDF is fit to the five demo documents, and M4 has no incremental-value comparison. Massive `adjusted=true` is split-adjusted price, not dividend total return. | Fit-corpus boundaries, point-in-time industry data, price and dividend source/version, missingness policy and comparable model capacity. | Which controls are primary; whether and how to define any financial target. |
| 6. Metrics and uncertainty | Pre-specify upper-triangle similarity rank agreement and per-company Top-k overlap; use appropriate firm/time units for uncertainty. | Add downstream peer-group or risk-model metrics after task choice. | Firm pairs are dependent; a five-company descriptive statistic cannot support a population claim. | Repeated filings/dates, perturbation arms, failure masks and pre-specified aggregation. | Primary metric, K for Top-k, uncertainty unit, thresholds and multiplicity policy. |
| 7. Price PCA K, if used | Select K by a pre-registered rule fitted only on past training-window returns; report rank and sensitivity. | Fixed K chosen before data or a training-only variance threshold. | M4 used **K=2** as an engineering-demo config; no historical selection rationale is recorded, and rank 5 did not force K=2. | Past-window return matrix, allowed training dates, rank/variance curves and rule implementation. | **Formal K rule remains TBD**; whether PCA belongs in the primary task. |

**Track gates:** [M5_TRACKS.md](M5_TRACKS.md) separates the proposed fixed-text mainline from optional historical return and risk extensions. Only the latter require a trading decision time and historical text–price alignment. Before any formal run, professor and researcher must approve a dated protocol with exact sample, model/data versions, controls, metrics, exclusions and amendment procedure. Keep held-out results untouched until those choices are frozen. M4's self-audit was written and run by the same agent, so it is not an independent review. See [DATA_CONTRACT.md](DATA_CONTRACT.md) and [the M4 closeout](../reports/2026-10-19_M4_closeout.md) for evidence limits.
