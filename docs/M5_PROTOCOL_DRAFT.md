## Round 7 proposed minimum financial extension

The [current machine-readable candidate](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round8.json) is `PROPOSED`, `researcher_accepted=false`, and `formal_financial_experiment_status=NOT_RUN`. It supersedes the **proposal bytes** in the unchanged [round-7 candidate](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round7.json), SHA-256 `f49c3800267250a21928c71e2aa0c82dc778195c82a02ec0722e05c59dc38c15`. This is a **retrospective fixed-model price-return association** with a chronological supervised holdout, not an as-of-tradable forecast. The previously fixed engineering 128-body-token, zero-overlap, equal-chunk MiniLM/ONNX reference is proposed because it predates the S2/S3 observed comparisons; neither their lowest vector distance nor their most favorable ranking statistic selects a new default. S2/S3 used five development texts and supply no independent financial validation set.

### Event, clock, label and scope

One unit is one original 10-K Item 1 section for one CIK and fiscal period, linked to one eligible decision session. The planning cohort is up to 200 CIKs drawn by the [candidate's exact deterministic hash rule](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round7.json) from a verified **as-of-2020-01-01** U.S. exchange-listed operating-common-share security master; keep later delistings and identifier changes. The five S2/S3 development CIKs are excluded from this candidate cohort. The 2020–2023 decision years form training, 2024 validation, and 2025 untouched test. This is a resource-bounded cohort target, not a sample-size or statistical-power claim. A bounded starting security extract and histories now have local receipts, but eligible operating-common-share/CIK membership and the broader original Item 1 inventory remain unverified; an admitted count must replace the planning count before protocol acceptance.

The proposed decision time is **18:00 America/New_York on the first regular trading session strictly after the official SEC filing date**. It requires point-in-time evidence that both the exact Item 1 version and that session's finalized close bar were available by 18:00, plus a specified post-acceptance-correction policy; otherwise the event is ineligible with a reason. The primary five-subsequent-session target is the simple split-adjusted **price** return `C(i,d+5)/C(i,d)-1`, from decision-session close `d` to close `d+5`, with the label available only after that later close and vendor finalization. The start close is a prediction reference, **not an assumed executable fill**. A 21-session horizon is a predeclared alternative, not a second result to select after seeing test outcomes. Neither target includes dividends. Total-return and delisting-aware claims need additional corporate-action data and a fixed reinvestment rule. A regular-session calendar, public-time evidence, exact text vintage, and the necessary price history are execution gates; missing events remain null with reasons, never zero-filled.

Use only 21 consecutive regular-session predecision closing prices ending at `d` for the three shared nontext features: `C(d)/C(d-5)-1`, `C(d)/C(d-20)-1`, and the population standard deviation (`ddof=0`) of `C(t)/C(t-1)-1` for the 20 sessions ending at `d`. This does not make the full 2025 M4 price array a predecision feature. Require the subsequent `d+5` close for the primary label, with all intervening sessions and actions checked. If prices are missing or a security exits before the horizon, retain the event and reason in the eligibility ledger. A broad-cohort evaluation is blocked until a delisting outcome rule and source cover exits; a narrower survivor estimand would require a separate explicit decision and disclosure. Do not zero-fill or silently select survivors. The present 2025 Massive bars are split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted, according to the [provider's aggregate-bar FAQ](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends). Their download vintage is later than the historical decision dates.

### Matched prediction arms and fit boundaries

Use the **same eligible event rows, labels, decision dates, exclusions and chronological splits** in all arms. `F0` contains the three trailing-price features. `F1` adds 32 coordinates from train-only Item 1 TF-IDF: lowercase `[a-z][a-z0-9]+` tokens, raw term counts, a maximum 5,000-term vocabulary chosen by descending training-document frequency with lexical ties, smoothed IDF `log((1+N)/(1+df))+1`, row L2 normalization, and train-only truncated SVD. `F2` adds 32 coordinates from train-only centered/unscaled PCA of the pinned 384-dimensional, 128/equal MiniLM filing vectors. The fixed transformations equalize the added coordinate count; they do not make lexical and dense information or effective model capacity identical. If training rank is below 32 or a required row has zero/undefined norm, fail or revise the still-unaccepted protocol before any test evaluation. The earlier M4 `K=2` centered price PCA and five-document TF-IDF were descriptive demonstrations, **not** an out-of-sample price-only prediction baseline.

Each arm uses an intercept ridge predictor with training-only mean/standard-deviation feature scaling (`ddof=0`) and the same `alpha={0.1,1,10}` search. Record and drop zero-variance coordinates based only on that fit window; do not fit an imputer on held-out data. Select by 2024 validation MSE, breaking exact ties toward the largest alpha. A training label used to fit before validation must end and be finalized **before the first validation decision**. Every validation label used for alpha selection must end and be finalized **before the first test decision**; late events are purged from validation, not merely omitted from refit. After selection, refit vocabulary, IDF, SVD/PCA, scalers and predictor on permitted training plus validation events under the same boundary; do not fit any transform on test texts, features or labels. Do not random-shuffle time, select a text setting on 2025 results, or repeat one filing vector on many daily rows and call them independent filing events.

Calculate predictions and metrics in float64. The primary held-out statistic is `1 - SSE(F2)/SSE(F0)` on the same 2025 filing events, with an undefined result if the F0 denominator is zero. Positive values mean lower squared error than the nontext baseline **within this specified retrospective comparison**. Secondary squared-error reductions are `1-SSE(F1)/SSE(F0)` and `1-SSE(F2)/SSE(F1)` on identical rows, null if the reference SSE is zero. Paired absolute-error differences are `MAE(F0)-MAE(F2)` and `MAE(F1)-MAE(F2)` on those rows, positive when F2 has lower error. For each decision calendar month, compute Spearman between predictions and labels only with at least five distinct eligible firms; if one firm has multiple eligible originals that month, use the earliest decision event for this secondary metric in every arm and log the others. Use average ranks for exact ties, set a constant-series month to null with reason, and equal-weight defined months in the summary. State every denominator, missing reason, firm/time dependence and exclusion; distinct filings from one company are not independent statistical draws. The test set is touched once under settings frozen **before** it is opened. No p-value, population claim, trading value or cost-adjusted return is pre-authorized. A separate trading protocol would have to freeze order timing, execution price, turnover, spread, fees, slippage, financing, delisting and dividend conventions before an economic result.

### Time evidence and resource boundary

The [exact pinned revision commit](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/commit/1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41) is a 2026-06-01 README metadata change. The matching [ONNX file](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/commit/bc57282bc374d33e0d6c4de27f12dc1c2a87f37a) was present by 2024-10-10, and the [official file-history tree](https://huggingface.co/api/models/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/tree/1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41?recursive=true&expand=true) places the matching tokenizer blob by 2021-08-30. The [model card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/blob/1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41/README.md) describes training pairs but gives no verified global training-data cutoff. The 2026 revision identifier does **not** mean its ONNX bytes first appeared in 2026. A strict historical 2025 design would need a contemporaneous complete revision/byte manifest, cutoff evidence, public filing and text-version evidence, and contemporaneous market data. Until those gates pass, the candidate's dense-arm interpretation stays retrospective; it cannot establish absence of look-ahead contamination. The ONNX file's model-repository history alone does not prove numerical equivalence to the original PyTorch weights.

At the illustrative one-filing-per-firm-year rate, 200 CIKs over six decision years imply 1,200 filing events before attrition; this is **not a hard cap or observed count**. The observed five development Item 1 sections had 216 total 128-token chunks, or 43.2 chunks per section; scaling that observed mean to 1,200 would give 51,840 chunks **only as a workload illustration**, not a time or memory prediction for new texts. One 1,200×384 float32 filing-vector array would occupy 1,843,200 bytes before metadata and intermediate vectors. Three arms and three alpha candidates plan nine validation fits plus three post-selection refits; actual eligible rows and fit failures are unknown. Actual corpus lengths, batch calls, runtime and peak memory require a future tokenizer-only preflight and measured pilot. No university GPU allocation is verified or required by this minimum CPU candidate.

Before any financial training or evaluation, obtain a version-bound research decision, point-in-time security/CIK links, exact historical text and public-time policy, complete price and delisting/corporate-action coverage, a regular-session calendar, model-time interpretation, and a frozen eligibility ledger. The [round-7 feasibility report](../reports/2026-11-01_s2_s3_m5_feasibility.md) records the current local evidence and ordered decisions. None of these gates was completed by S2/S3 model inference.

## Round 8 proposed execution clarifications, not acceptance

The [round-8 candidate JSON](../configs/m5_financial_protocol_round8.json) makes the earlier numerical rules executable without changing its five-session primary, 21-session alternative, F0/F1/F2 comparison, 128/equal dense reference, chronological years, 32-dimensional text arms or `alpha={0.1,1,10}` suggestion. The new [date-coverage audit](../scripts/audit_m5_horizon_round8.py) reads those **two** horizons from the candidate. Its local receipt describes saved common-date-axis positions only: without a separately verified exchange calendar, `d+h` is not certified as the h-th exchange session and no financial event is admitted. A missing bar on the reference axis blocks the pair and never shifts `d+h` to a later available price. The old round-7 20-session availability field remains a preserved historical check, not this candidate's 21-session alternative.

**Security join.** The prediction event is a CIK filing linked at decision time to **one stable, listed common-share security identifier**, its effective-date interval and ticker history. Prefer a unique point-in-time primary listing designation. If multiple share classes remain without a unique documented primary security, record `AMBIGUOUS_SECURITY` and exclude the event under the common three-arm mask; do not choose by future price/label coverage. A separately approved alternative could rank classes by predecision 60-session dollar volume, changing the selection rule and required data. Keep exits and identifier changes; no survivor-only silent filter.

**Lexical transformation.** Lowercase and tokenize Item 1 with `[a-z][a-z0-9]+`, without stemming or stopword removal. An actually empty tokenized section fails the common event admission. Fit vocabulary on the allowed training documents only: descending document frequency, alphabetical ties, at most 5,000 terms. Use raw term counts, `IDF=log((1+N)/(1+df))+1`, then L2-normalize each TF-IDF row. A validation/test document with no terms in the training vocabulary yields a recorded `OOV_ONLY` zero lexical row and zero SVD coordinates; it is not silently replaced with a different text. A zero training TF-IDF row blocks the fit. SVD is **uncentered** full-matrices-false float64 `numpy.linalg.svd` on the training matrix; future rows use `X @ V_32.T`. No randomized solver or seed is used. Flip each component so its largest absolute loading is positive, breaking loading-index ties toward the smallest index.

**Dense transform and numerical rank.** The pinned 384-coordinate filing vector is converted to float64. Fit PCA by subtracting **training-only column means**, with no coordinate scaling, then deterministic full-matrices-false float64 SVD; future rows use `(X-training_mean) @ V_32.T`. Both lexical SVD and dense PCA require finite singular values and numerical rank at least 32 under `max(n_rows,n_columns) * eps(float64) * largest_singular_value`; a 32/33 boundary indistinguishable at that tolerance, SVD nonconvergence or insufficient rank blocks the candidate. Use the same deterministic component-sign rule as above. A smaller cohort is **not automatically feasible** with 32 components; count eligible training rows and rank before retaining this representation rule. Fixed Encoder reruns are runtime checks, not independent training seeds.

**Scaling, Ridge and failure policy.** Fit mean and population standard deviation (`ddof=0`) only on the current training window, separately for each arm. Record and drop zero-variance columns from that fit window; repeat fitting on permitted train+validation at final refit, never on test. Use float64 Ridge with an unpenalized intercept and objective `sum_i(y_i-intercept-x_i·beta)^2 + alpha*sum_j(beta_j^2)` on standardized coordinates. Thus `alpha` penalizes a **summed**, not averaged, squared error. Solve with deterministic float64 `numpy.linalg.lstsq(rcond=None)` on an augmented design carrying `sqrt(alpha)` coefficient penalty rows and a zero intercept penalty; no randomized seed or outcome-selected repeat is proposed. Nonfinite values, an empty eligible split, undefined reference SSE, numerical failure or a missing required arm produce a named failure/null, never a complete three-arm score or filled zero. The existing validation and test chronology, tie rules and same-row metrics remain as stated above.

**Time-evidence classes.** A proven *first-public instant* would require an independent time-stamped distribution/archive record; SEC acceptance is a different event and SEC does not publish a first-web-availability timestamp. An independent capture or official dissemination record may instead establish a **no-later-than** bound, without proving the first instant. A dated filing-plus-lag rule accepted by the researcher is an **availability proxy**, not an observed publication timestamp. The proposed 18:00 Eastern text-and-finalized-close gate is currently unmet because the saved 2026 SEC/price snapshots do not establish both facts by historical 18:00. A stricter as-of study requires those bounds, contemporaneous model-file identity/training-knowledge evidence and point-in-time market data; a fixed-snapshot retrospective study can proceed only under its explicitly narrower interpretation after its own decision and data gates. The [round-8 source report](../reports/2026-11-01_m5_execution_and_sources_round8.md) records the official documentation, local access status and minimum acquisition decision path.
